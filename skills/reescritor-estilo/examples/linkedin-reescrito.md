We just launched Beacon, a new open-source tool for accessibility testing.

It came out of 8 months spent looking at the problems developers run into every day when they try to make a product accessible. Accessibility is a legal requirement. It's also the right thing to do, and the products that get it right end up more inclusive and more usable, and they tend to do better.

The goal was a tool that's fast, reliable and easy to use. Beacon already checks 42 WCAG 2.2 criteria and plugs straight into GitHub Actions and GitLab CI.

Big thanks to Priya and Tom, and to everyone else on the team. We couldn't have done it without you.
